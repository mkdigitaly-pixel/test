# UTM-метки

Базовый формат: `?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=medium&utm_campaign=CAMPAIGN`

| Источник | utm_source | utm_medium | Пример campaign |
|----------|------------|------------|-----------------|
| Дзен | dzen | article | penoplast-case |
| Telegram | telegram | post | autop targeting-b2b |
| VK | vk | post | penoplast-case |
| VC.ru | vc | article | penoplast-case |

Пример:
`https://mkekspert.ru/razbor-direct?utm_source=dzen&utm_medium=article&utm_campaign=penoplast-case`
